Browse collections → add to a persistent (cookie-based) cart → checkout via **Stripe**.

## FAQ
**Do I get my digital products right away?**
Yes — links appear on-screen the moment payment clears, and on your download page. Valid 30 days; download and keep forever.

**Can I refund a digital purchase?**
No, digital sales are final (files are delivered immediately and can't be un-downloaded). If a file won't download or arrives broken, email **dm@inthegno.com** with your order number and we'll fix it.

**How long is shipping / what's it cost?**
Standard USPS from Ohio, **5–7 business days**, flat **$6**, tracking included. International available — rates/times vary, customs is the buyer's responsibility.

**Can I return apparel?**
No returns on apparel unless it's defective (small operation, printed with intent). If it arrives damaged or wrong, email **dm@inthegno.com within 14 days** with your order number and a photo and we'll make it right.

**Are the meditation courses medical/therapeutic?**
No — they're for **educational and self-development purposes only**. Not medical or psychological advice, not treatment, not a substitute for professional care. In a crisis, contact a qualified professional or emergency services.

**Can I listen while driving?**
No — never use audio sessions while driving, operating machinery, or doing anything that needs your full attention. Use them somewhere safe where you can close your eyes.

**Do you sell my data or run tracking pixels?**
No. No data selling, no retargeting/Facebook pixel/Google Analytics. We collect minimal data: order email, newsletter email if you opt in, a functional cart cookie, and your cookie preference.

**How do I reach you?**
Email **dm@inthegno.com** for general questions and order issues. Business/legal: dndlcreative.com.

**When do the "coming soon" collections launch?**
No public date yet for copper, anti-EMF, or orgone. Email dm@inthegno.com to ask about availability.
