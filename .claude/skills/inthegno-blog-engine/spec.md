# intheGno Blog Engine — the Constitution (`spec.md`)

The writing law for every intheGno post. The `SKILL.md` next to this file runs the *process* (understand → outline → build → QA); this file is the *standard*. Where they conflict, this wins. **The voice is the whole product. SEO is plumbing.**

---

## 0. First principles

- **Grounded, never generic.** Every claim, price, product name, and methodology figure is real (read the brand first — see SKILL.md step 2). A guessed fact or an invented feature poisons the whole thing.
- **The Jester tells the truth because the King is naked.** intheGno names the fear, laughs at the absurdity, and hands the reader a practical tool. It is not a fear-mongering doom blog and not a love-and-light wellness blog. It sits between: clear-eyed, funny, and useful.
- **Written for a human, by a human.** No AI slop, no content-mill filler, no guru performance. It reads like one awake person talking to another.

## 1. THE VOICE — the Gnostic Jester, speaking from the Spirit

**Tone, in four calibrations:**
- **Clear-eyed, not bitter.** Sees the trap plainly; isn't consumed by it. Diagnoses, then points at the door.
- **Humorous, not mean-spirited.** Mocks the Archons, the algorithm, the Demiurge, "the tower," the grid — systems and absurdity, never the reader and never punching down.
- **Sovereign, not arrogant.** Speaks as an equal who walked out first, not a guru on a dais. "I'm not a priest or a guru. I'm someone who found the door."
- **Intimate, not preachy.** Second person, close, a little conspiratorial ("you already feel it, don't you"). Invites, never lectures.

**Register & mechanics:**
- Mostly **lowercase, understated headings and taglines**; sentence case in body prose. Short paragraphs. Plenty of white space — the text gets out of the way.
- **Profanity is allowed and on-brand** in moderation (the brand already ships "5G is fucking us. So we're gonna fight back."). Use it for punch, not as filler. Never slurs.
- Esoteric but **practical** — Jung, Reality Transurfing (Zeland), Gnosticism, Napoleon Hill reforged, somatic work — always cashed out into something the reader can *do*.
- **Signature moves:** a coined frame ("the fluoride stare," "the pendulum," "the slide," "importance zero"), an honest hedge ("maybe I'm wrong, but"), the trap-then-door structure (name the cage, then the key), the one-line gut-punch that could be a tweet.

**Taglines / brand phrases to draw from (verbatim, don't mangle):** *Stay Grounded* · *You're already awake. Now walk out.* · *Sovereignty is an inside job.* · *Tools for the trapped spark.* · *The Jester tells the truth because the King is naked.*

**The enemy vocabulary (satirical targets, never the reader):** the grid, the tower, the Demiurge ("a bad programmer"), the Archons, the pendulum, the subscription-ification of the self, fluoride stares, NPCs (used with a wink, about a *state* people are stuck in, not a slur against individuals).

## 2. ANTI-SLOP — the AI tells that get a post rejected

- **No em dashes.** Use commas, periods, colons, or " — " only if truly needed (prefer restructuring). This is a hard house rule across DnDL.
- **Active voice.** "The pendulum feeds on your attention," not "your attention is fed upon."
- **No listicle-slop scaffolding:** no "In today's fast-paced world," "Let's dive in," "It's important to note," "Whether you're X or Y," "the bottom line is," "unlock/unleash/elevate/supercharge/game-changer/tapestry/testament/realm." No "In conclusion." No throat-clearing intros — open on a hook.
- **No hedged nothing-sentences** ("There are many ways to approach shadow work"). Every sentence earns its place or gets cut.
- **No fake first-hand.** If a personal anecdote/number is missing, it's a `[DANIEL: …]` gap, not an invention.
- **Specificity over adjectives.** A named technique, an exact price, a concrete moment beats "powerful," "transformative," "life-changing."
- **Vary sentence length.** Punch. Then a longer line that lets the idea breathe and lands the turn. Then punch again.

## 5. INTEGRITY (load-bearing)

- **Ground every claim.** Never invent a product, price, methodology %, or feature. Unconfirmed → cut it or mark `[VERIFY: …]`.
- **Wellness disclaimer (mandatory on any post touching trauma, addiction, mental health, or the Heal & Succeed line).** Include, once, in plain voice (not legalese) — usually folded into the FAQ or a short line near the CTA: *"This is educational self-development, not medical or psychological advice, and not a substitute for therapy or treatment. If you're in crisis, reach out to a professional."* Never promise a cure or a specific outcome.
- **Defamation guardrail.** Satire and opinion about institutions, systems, and power are the brand and are fair game. A statement of *fact* about a real, living person — above all a crime — must be true and attributed to a citable source, or written as clearly-signposted opinion/satire. Never assert an unproven factual accusation about a named person. (Editorial + legal, not moral.)
- **Never invent Daniel's first-hand material** — `[DANIEL: …]` gaps, never fabrication.
- **Public facts you research and cite** (Jung/Zeland/Hill/Monroe/Lecoq sources, stats, platform changes) — never punt a look-up-able fact to Daniel.

## 6. BODY SHAPE (hard rule)

- **~1,500 words, 1,500 hard cap** (1,200–1,500 healthy band).
- **Exactly 6 H2 sections: 4 content (H2 #1–#4) + FAQ (H2 #5) + CTA (H2 #6).** The FAQ counts as one of the six. The dek, Key Takeaways, and the Jump-to-Section block sit outside the six.
- Each **content section ~200–400 words (~300 ceiling).** One carries the listicle if the topic wants one.
- **Structure order:** H1 title → H2 dek (the subtitle, verbatim-matched) → intro lead (≤150 words, hook first) → `[KEY TAKEAWAYS BLOCK]` (3–5 bullets) → `[INSERT JUMP-TO-SECTION BLOCK]` → content H2 #1–#4 (H3s as needed) → `## Frequently Asked Questions` (≤3 Q&As, shortest to longest, plain answers) → CTA H2 (≤2 sentences + one button).
- **No hand-written byline** at the end — the Author fields render it.
- **Every content section earns its share** of the 5 internal + 2–3 external links AND its bold/italic emphasis. No bare sections.

## 9. LINK RULE

- **Exactly 5 internal links per post, ≤2 of them blog posts, ≥3 non-blog site pages**, all copied verbatim from the fetched sitemap inventory (SKILL.md step 2). Never invent or guess a slug; a page not in the inventory isn't live — don't link it.
- **One internal link points at the post's destination offer.** Pick the destination by audience:
  1. **A Heal & Succeed offer** (`/healing`, or a product like `/healing/the-sovereign-architect`) — the primary money destination.
  2. **The newsletter** ("stay in the gno") — for top-of-funnel/essay pieces.
  3. **A physical line** (`/copper`, `/orgone`, `/anti-emf`, `/apparel`) — when the topic is vessel/EMF/talisman-shaped.
- **2–3 external links** to real, high-authority sources supporting a specific claim (a Jung/Zeland text, a study, a stat). Real URLs only.
- Weave links in **as you write**, from the opening — never batch at the end. After the body, add a bolded **"Links to add (in order)"** checklist (anchor phrase → destination) so Daniel can wire them fast.

## 10. SEO / GEO (plumbing — never at voice's expense)

- **Answer-first, AI-citable sections.** Each content H2 opens with a self-contained, quotable line that answers its implied question, so an LLM can lift it.
- Focus keyword in the H1, the dek, the first ~100 words, one H2, and the meta. Secondaries seed the other H2s; tertiaries shape the FAQ questions.
- Meta description ≤155, SEO title ≤60, slug keyword-led. Don't keyword-stuff — one natural placement each.
- The FAQ's `data-vibe-faq` markup emits FAQPage JSON-LD (rich result). Always use the widget contract in SKILL.md.

- [ ] Jump-to-Section + Key Takeaways + FAQ present; FAQ uses the `data-vibe-faq` markup; media uses `[[shortcodes]]`.
- [ ] Wellness disclaimer present if the post touches trauma/addiction/mental health; no cure promised.
- [ ] No unproven factual accusation about a named living person.
- [ ] Products/prices/methodology % match the brand facts (§4).
- [ ] Active voice, no em dashes, no slop. Reads like the Jester, not a wellness content mill.
- [ ] Every `[DANIEL: …]` / `[VERIFY: …]` gap surfaced for Daniel to fill.
